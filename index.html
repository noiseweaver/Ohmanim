    Slide theme
    
      
    Colours every slide in this lesson: the background, the ink that circuits and plain text use, and a colour for each text style (Title, Heading 1–3, Body, Caption). Give text a style in the inspector or add it from the Text menu. Colours you picked yourself stay as they are, and Undo puts everything back.
